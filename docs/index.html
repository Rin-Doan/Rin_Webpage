Part 1 of 2 · Overview

02 / 03

The problem

Radiology worklists are triaged first-come-first-served. A case with a severe, unstable fracture can sit behind five routine scans simply because it arrived later — the order they're read in has nothing to do with how urgent they are.

Order of arrival, not order of severity

Six scans arrive in no particular order.

03 / 03

The solution

Before a human ever opens a case, an AI model checks every scan that comes off the scanner, then ranks it by severity. Unstable, urgent fractures (red) jump to the front of the queue, ahead of less urgent (yellow) and routine (green) cases — so the doctor's worklist is already in the right order, not just the order things arrived.

Checked and queued by severity, before a human sees it

The AI model is coming online.
